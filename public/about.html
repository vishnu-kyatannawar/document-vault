Document Vault

Your documents, secured in your own Google Drive.

Keep your important documents one tap away

Document Vault is a free, installable web app for storing, viewing and sharing personal documents — ID cards, driving licences, vehicle papers, insurance policies, certificates and PDFs — from your phone or computer.

Open the app Source code

What it does

  • Add documents from your camera, photo gallery or files, with multiple pages per document (for example the front and back of a licence).
  • Organise them into groups and nested folders such as Car or Family.
  • View images and PDFs in the app, with pinch-to-zoom.
  • Get reminders before a document expires, and keep notes with each one.
  • Download or share a page to WhatsApp or any other app.
  • Share with people: give another Google account live, read-only access to a group or document — one copy, always current.
  • Back up everything to a single file, optionally password-protected, and restore it anywhere.

How it uses your Google account

There is no Document Vault server. When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to create and manage files in a Document Vault folder in your own Google Drive (Google's drive.file permission — it cannot see any other file in your Drive) and to show your name, email address and profile picture so you can tell which account is signed in. Your documents are uploaded from your device straight to your Drive and stay there; you can open them in Google Drive at any time.

Full details are in the privacy policy.

Open source

The app is open source. You can read every line of code, run it yourself with your own Google Cloud project, or report a problem on GitHub.
