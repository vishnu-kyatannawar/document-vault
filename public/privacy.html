Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Document Vault is designed so that your documents never leave your own Google account. This page explains exactly what the app accesses, where data is kept, and how you stay in control.

The short version

  • There is no Document Vault server. The app runs entirely in your browser and talks only to Google's APIs.
  • Your documents are stored in a Document Vault folder in your own Google Drive, and nowhere else.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or analyse your data. There are no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers.
  • You can delete everything at any time, and revoke the app's access from your Google account.

What the app asks Google for

When you sign in with Google, the app requests these permissions:

  • Basic profile (openid, email, profile): your name, email address and profile picture, used only to show which account is signed in and to suggest your account on the next sign-in.
  • Google Drive, files created by this app only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): lets the app create the Document Vault folder in your Drive and read, update, share and delete the folders and files it created there. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

How your data is used

The pages you add (photos, scans, PDFs) are uploaded from your device directly to your Google Drive and are read back from there when you view, download or share them. Document titles, dates, expiry reminders and notes are stored as properties on those Drive folders. The app never sends any of this to anyone other than Google Drive on your behalf.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you see in the app, is never transferred to third parties (except as needed to provide those features, or as required by law), is never used for advertising, and is never read by a human.

Sharing with people

If you choose to share a group or document with someone, the app adds a standard Google Drive viewer permission for that person's Google account on the corresponding folder. Nothing is copied; the other person sees the files in your Drive with the access level you chose. You can see and remove that access in the app or in Google Drive at any time. Google may email the recipient a notification of the share, as it does for any Drive sharing.

What is stored on your device

  • A short-lived Google access token (valid for about one hour), its expiry, and your name, email and profile picture, so that reopening the app does not require signing in again. These are removed when you sign out.
  • Small preview thumbnails of your pages, cached so lists load quickly.
  • A local diagnostic log (timestamps and error messages, never document contents) that only you can download from the profile screen.
  • The app's own files, cached so it can open offline.

All of this lives in your browser's storage for this site only and never leaves your device.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Delete documents or groups inside the app, or delete the Document Vault folder in Google Drive — the app has no other copy.
  • Sign out in the app to clear the cached token and profile from your device.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your files remain in your Drive.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
